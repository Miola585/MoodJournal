About the Event

A safe corner for your feelings. Jot down your mood, add a note, build healthy habits and come back to see how you are growing. No Pressure! Reflect on what matters and find small ways to feel better everyday!

What to Expect

  • Journaling: Express your emotions freely
  • Emotion Identification: Find the words to describe how you feel
  • Activities: Be prepared to move to improve your mood

Event Schedule

Emotion Exploration

Take a look at the wheel and try to identify your current mood. Use the check in button to pick out which emotion best represents how you are feeling

Journals and Notes

Be bold! Take some time to write out how you are feeling and feel free to express your thoughts, emotions, or events that have previously happened.

Mindfulness Activity

Activities to help lift or steady your mood. You'll get activities recommended to you based on your current emotion. Pick out one or two to help improve your mood.

Stay Connected

Stay connected with upcoming wellness activities, journaling prompts, and mindfulness tips. Join our community to receive gentle reminders and supportive resources to help you reflect, reset, and take care of your well-being.
