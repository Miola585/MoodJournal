STICKER-SPECIFIC NOTES
----------------------
Geometric sun
  Use mostly lemon yellow. Add only a narrow muted-gold lower shadow and one
  small pale-yellow highlight. This should be the cleanest, simplest sun.
  Keep the large triangular rays because they remain readable when small.

Smiling sun
  Keep the face bright gold, the rays visibly orange, and the facial details
  brown. Use the pale cream only as a soft highlight around the face edge.
  Keep the eyes and mouth simple enough to read at 64-96px in the sticker book.

Special sun
  Keep the center almost entirely bright yellow, with a narrow golden edge.
  Build the flames from vivid orange and amber, then use muted ochre only on
  their shadow edges and tips. The source does not use a cream flame highlight.

Soft cloud
  Use the middle-right compact cloud from the vector reference for the standalone
  sticker. Use the middle-left layered cloud as the quieter in-game sky element.
  Simplify small interior lines and use solid pixel-art fills.
